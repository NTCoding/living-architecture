# design-trade-off

This is not a role. It's a declaration that is used to communicate why a design choice was made, one that may seem to violate project principles. MUST be approved by a human before it is added.

## Rules

- Always pairs with a `@riviere-role`. It never replaces one.
- The comment must name the principle being deviated from and say why the deviation is the better call.
- Any PR containing `@riviere-design-trade-off` is flagged in the architecture diff so a human reviews the trade-off explicitly.
