# PRD

Draft `PRD.md` once solution exploration is approved.

## What the document must answer

- What we are building, from the point of view of the person who uses it.
- What we are not building.
- The success criteria, as things that can be checked.
- The terminology, using the words of the glossary.
- The open questions that only Nick can answer.

## Checklist before you tell Nick it is ready

- [ ] Every requirement is testable.
- [ ] The words match `definitions.glossary.yml`.
- [ ] The out of scope list is not empty.
- [ ] Each open question is a `:::decision` block.
